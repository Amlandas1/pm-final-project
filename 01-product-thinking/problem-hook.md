# Problem Hook & Value Proposition (Module 1)

## Responses
- **Chosen path, which scenario are you committing to? (StreamLine or RouteLogic, or your own):** StreamLine
- **Strategic crisis, based on the brief, if the company does nothing for 12 months, what happens to its market position?:** Market position will be very weak in best case in worst case it could be none.
- **Moment of misery, what is the user likely doing outside the product (Google, spreadsheets, a competitor) because your tool isn’t serving them?:** Consumers do not view the platform as their primary entertainment home, but keep it as a low-friction "second screen" or background-viewing option alongside a prestige primary service.In worst case scenario the platform becomes completely interchangeable with free, ad-supported streaming TV (FAST) services. Viewers see no reason to pay a monthly fee for mid-tier content they can get elsewhere for free.
- **Problem hook, summarize the business risk and user pain into one urgent sentence.:** By operating as an uncurated warehouse of 15,000 mid-tier titles, the platform triggers user choice paralysis and erodes perceived value—causing high-LTV, quality-seeking viewers to churn and leaving the business trapped in a low-margin commodity position with zero pricing power.
- **Value proposition, based on the proposed initiative (Spotlight or Velocity), what is the new value and why is it urgent to launch now?:** New Value: Spotlight replaces endless scrolling with trusted, human-led curation that saves time and guarantees high-craft entertainment—transforming the platform from a generic content warehouse into a cultural tastemaker that maximizes return on viewer attention. Why Now: With households aggressively cutting redundant subscriptions and free FAST channels owning the "volume" tier, launching immediately is critical to establish editorial differentiation and lock in high-LTV, quality-driven viewers before competitors claim the space.
